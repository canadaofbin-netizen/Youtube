## 3. Clips 4–10 Progression (Replacing 6 Fake Brain Clips with Real Experimental Variations)

- **Clip 4 (`crisis` — Scene `S5`):** Replaces the fake *"anterior insula bottleneck"* brain dive with the **secret staged setup**: the rigged paper-slip draw (both slips reading *"Teacher"*), the actor Learner, and the **real 45-volt sample shock** on the Teacher's own wrist (`M2` blocking).
- **Clip 5 (`pivot` — Scene `S4`):** Replaces the fake *"prefrontal hierarchical command relay"* with the verified **Haslam & Reicher (2012) / Burger et al. (2011) prod twist**: volunteers continued when told *"The experiment requires that you continue"* (Prod 2, appeal to science), but **stopped and shoved their chairs back** when given the bare order *"You have no other choice"* (Prod 4).
- **Clip 6 (`step1` — Scene `S6`):** Same-room proximity variation (`Milgram 1965`) — removing the partition wall so the Teacher sees the Learner struggle in the same room drops obedience from **65% to 40%**.
- **Clip 7 (`step2` — Scene `S3`):** Touch-proximity variation (`Milgram 1965`) — forcing the Teacher to physically press the Learner's hand onto the wired steel contact plate drops obedience to **30%**.
- **Clip 8 (`step3` — Scene `S4`):** Dissenting peers variation (`Experiment 17`) — when **two peer teachers refuse to flip the switches** and push their chairs back, **36 of 40 men** stop too.
- **Clip 9 (`breakthrough` — Scene `S8`):** Jerry Burger's **2009 replication** (`doi:10.1037/a0010932`) stopped ethically at the **150-volt** tenth switch, where **70%** still tried to continue.
- **Clip 10 (`conclusion` — Scene `S10`):** Connects directly to the viewer: a calm voice claiming *"it is for a good cause"* is the real trap, across all 30 versions most people stopped, and you can push your chair back and walk away too.
