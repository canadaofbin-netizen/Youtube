## R40-A — Schema v4 Prompt Structure (`50–80 Words`, 3 Sentences)

Every clip prompt generated by `build_flow_prompts()` in [`pipeline/flow_prompt_builder.py`](file:///home/canadaofbin/gdrive/Kyubin_Yun_Workspace/07_Youtube/pipeline/flow_prompt_builder.py) follows the exact 3-part structure:

```text
Generate a 9:16 vertical video clip (video animation with spoken voiceover only, never a static image): {3-sentence visual scene (35-55 words)}. Voice: Consistent deep male baritone documentary narrator. Spoken narration: "{narration (15-26 words)}"
```

### 10-Clip Spatial & Role Spine (`Schema v4`)
- **Clip 1 (`setup`)**: Minimalist bright indoor studio/clinical/lab room + 1–2 concrete props + clothed matte-white porcelain mannequin performing a **2-Beat physical action** (`Beat 1` active verb + prop manipulation → `Beat 2` immediate physical consequence/reaction). Wide establishing shot tracking forward.
- **Clip 2 (`symptom`)**: Close-up on the porcelain face/upper body performing a **2-Beat physical symptom action** while the camera slowly orbits the head, revealing the target region (`electric cyan-blue`) and crisis region (`crimson-red`) through the translucent frosted-glass cranium.
- **Clip 3 (`failure`)**: In the same minimalist bright room, supporting mannequins interact with the subject as the subject performs a **2-Beat physical failure/rejection action** with a prop. Camera tracks smoothly sideways.
- **Clip 4 (`crisis`)**: Camera dives smoothly through the translucent frosted-glass cranium into a **bright white studio gallery** holding a realistic matte-white brain with visible gyri and sulci, zooming into the `electric cyan-blue` target region and `crimson-red` lesion/crisis region.
- **Clip 5 (`pivot`)**: Inside the matte-white brain in a **bright white studio gallery**, camera orbits the `electric cyan-blue` target and `crimson-red` crisis regions as the intact compensatory pathway ignites `warm amber-gold`.
- **Clip 6 (`step1`)**: Macro view inside the matte-white brain in a bright white studio gallery. `Bioluminescent green signal particles` flow along the `signal_path` into the `electric cyan-blue` hub as the camera tracks the flowing signal.
- **Clip 7 (`step2`)**: Inside the matte-white brain in a bright white studio gallery, camera follows the `bioluminescent green signal` to the `crimson-red` crisis junction where particles collide and scatter, then orbits the junction.
- **Clip 8 (`step3`)**: Camera pulls back to reveal the whole circuit (`electric cyan-blue`, `crimson-red`, and `warm amber-gold` routing a clean `bioluminescent green signal stream`) as the full brain turns slowly in the bright white studio gallery.
- **Clip 9 (`breakthrough`)**: Camera pulls smoothly out of the brain, back through the translucent frosted-glass cranium, into the bright minimalist room where the porcelain mannequin performs a **2-Beat physical breakthrough action** while the compensatory region glows `warm amber-gold` inside its cranium.
- **Clip 10 (`conclusion`)**: Wide shot of the bright minimalist room as the porcelain mannequin performs a **2-Beat composed physical action** alongside supporting mannequins, with `electric cyan-blue` and `warm amber-gold` visible inside its translucent frosted-glass cranium as the camera pulls back and rises.

## R5 — Credit-Aware Dual-Tier Engine Policy: Google Flow (`Omni 1.1 Flash` -> `0-Credit` Auto-Fallback & Batching)
- **Primary Engine (when AI credits > 0)**: **Google Flow `Omni 1.1 Flash`** (`FLOW_PRIMARY_VIDEO_MODEL`, `omni_1_1_flash`) in the visible Naver Whale browser.
- **Zero-Credit Video Auto-Fallback & Adaptive Batching**: Automatically switches across active shard tabs to **`Veo 3.1 - Lite [Lower Priority]`** (`FLOW_ZERO_CREDIT_MODEL`, `veo_3_1_lite_low_priority`, verified `"Generating will use 0 credits"`), scheduling `(n_worker_tabs, zero_credit_output_count)` via `compute_zero_credit_batch_plan` so `n_worker_tabs * zero_credit_output_count <= FLOW_ZERO_CREDIT_MAX_CONCURRENT_RENDERS` (up to `FLOW_ZERO_CREDIT_VIDEO_MAX_BATCH` candidate videos per prompt with CV best-of-batch selection). Never clicks `Add AI credits` top-up checkout links.
- **Zero-Credit Native Flow Image Batching (`DEFAULT_REENACT_IMAGE_BACKEND = "flow"`)**: Reference & `R67` porcelain keyframe generation runs directly inside Google Flow `Image` mode (`FLOW_ZERO_CREDIT_IMAGE_MODEL` / `FLOW_ZERO_CREDIT_IMAGE_FAMILY_ID`, verified `"Generating will use 0 credits"`) at `FLOW_ZERO_CREDIT_IMAGE_BATCH_COUNT` outputs per prompt, automatically scoring all candidates on `Q1–Q7` + `RQ2 dHash` and selecting the highest-scoring PNG.
- Dynamic clip duration routing (`compute_clip_target_duration`): `8s` for `neuro_diorama` / concise hooks / reference-conditioned clips, `10s` for native-voice Omni mechanism clips (`cutoff_risk == False`).
- Zero paid external APIs (`ALLOW_EXTERNAL_PAID_APIS = False`) and zero static slideshow fallbacks (`ALLOW_STATIC_IMAGE_VIDEO_FALLBACK = False`).

## R14-B — Banned Prompt Tokens (`BANNED_PROMPT_TOKENS`)
- **Structural bans**: `mobius`, `seamless loop`, `loop back`, `locked-off`, `tripod`, `5% push`, `decoupled kinetic`, `HUD`, `telemetry`, `mute-test`, `ARRI`, `Alexa`, `all about brain`, `watermark`, `#hex` codes, `Camera Angle N`.
- **Metaphor prop bans**: `clockwork`, `gear`, `gears`, `tape-reel`, `tape recorder`, `brass scale`, `balance scale`, `camera shutter`, `coin`, `coins`, `dollar bill`, `dollar bills`.

## R40-B — Sanitizer & Character Motion Validation (`sanitize_prompt_for_flow`, `validate_character_motion`)
- `sanitize_prompt_for_flow` strips historical proper names from visual scene descriptions (preserving spoken narration untouched).
- `validate_character_motion(prompts)` (`validate_flow_prompts(prompts, check_character_motion=True)`) verifies that every real-room character clip (`setup`, `symptom`, `failure`, `breakthrough`, `conclusion`) contains an active physical hand/arm/body action verb rather than a static pose (`stands nearby`, `sits upright`, `sits calmly`).

## R40-C — `visual_plan.json` Schema (v4)
- Required top-level keys: `real_environment`, `environment_props`, `scale_anchor_object`, `cast` (`subject`, `others`), `mannequin_actions` (`setup`, `symptom`, `failure`, `breakthrough`, `conclusion` — each `<= 14 words` with a 2-Beat physical action), `target_region`, `crisis_region`, `crisis_lesion`, `compensatory_region`, `signal_path` (3–5 structures), `mechanism_steps` (3 strings), and optional `scenes` overrides.
