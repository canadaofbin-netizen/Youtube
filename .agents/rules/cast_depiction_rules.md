Single source of truth for depicting all human and animal figures across ALL ABOUT BRAIN video clips.

## R14-A (Cast) — Clothed Articulated Matte-White Porcelain Mannequins with Translucent Frosted-Glass Craniums
- **Matte-White Porcelain Surface**: Every character in every real-room scene (`Clip 1, 2, 3, 9, 10`) is an **articulated matte-white porcelain mannequin** with a smooth featureless porcelain face and ball-jointed limbs.
- **Translucent Frosted-Glass Cranium (`31_O2CmlKOk` Signature Identity)**:
  - Each subject mannequin features a **translucent frosted-glass cranium** (`its translucent frosted-glass cranium catching the light`) that allows the camera to reveal internal neural state changes (`electric cyan-blue`, `crimson-red`, `warm amber-gold`) in `Clip 2, 9, 10` and dive smoothly through the cranium in `Clip 4` (`crisis`) and pull out in `Clip 9` (`breakthrough`).
- **Simple Role-Matched Wardrobe**: Mannequins wear clean, recognizable role clothing (`clothed railroad foreman mannequin`, `physician mannequin`, `researcher mannequin`) without cluttered historical crowd extras.
- **Expressive Body & Hand Acting**: Because porcelain faces are smooth and featureless, all emotion, tension, frustration, and relief are expressed through **2-Beat physical hand, arm, and posture actions** manipulating props (`scene_depiction_rules.md`).
- **Porcelain Animals**: When an experiment involves an animal (e.g., Pavlov's conditioning), render either a physiology subject mannequin or an articulated matte-white porcelain animal figure interacting cleanly with the table apparatus.
